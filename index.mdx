## Setup in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop the Jar">
    Download `JPPrison-<version>.jar` and place it inside your server's `plugins/` folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart the Server">
    Start (or restart) your server. JPPrison generates its full configuration tree under `plugins/JPPrison/` on first boot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure & Reload">
    Open the generated config files, tune them to your server's needs, and run `/jpprison reload` to apply your changes without rebooting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
