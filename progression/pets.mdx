Pets are floating head companions that follow you through the prison, providing passive economy and XP boosts as they level up. The more you mine with a pet equipped, the stronger it becomes — making pets a long-term investment that pays off the deeper you go into your sentence.

## Available Pets

JPPrison includes **13 pets**, each with a unique floating-head design. All textures are stored offline so skins never break on server restart — your pet looks the same whether the Mojang skin servers are reachable or not.

<Info>
  The `boomber` pet is free and available to every player by default — no unlock required.
</Info>

## Pet Menu

Open the pet menu with `/pets` or `/pet`. From the menu you can:

- **Equip** a pet to start following you
- **Unequip** your active pet
- **Customize** your pet's armor and particle trail

## Pet Levels

Pets level up automatically as you mine with them equipped. Higher levels grant stronger economy and XP boost multipliers. All level thresholds and boost values are configured in `plugins/JPPrison/pets.yml`.

<Tip>
  Keep a pet equipped during long mining sessions — passive level-up progress adds up fast and unlocks noticeably stronger boosts at higher tiers.
</Tip>

## Boosts

Each pet provides per-economy boost multipliers that apply to your earnings while the pet is equipped. Boosts scale with pet level.

**Example:** a Tokens pet at level 10 might grant **+25% token earnings** on every block you mine.

Because boosts are per-economy, different pets complement different playstyles — a Money pet benefits players focused on the sell economy, while a Tokens pet is ideal for upgrade grinders.

## Mini Armor

Pets can wear custom armor sets, giving them a fully kitted-out look as they float beside you. To access the pet armor customization menu you need the `jpprison.pet.armor` permission. Armor colors and available sets are configured in `pets.yml`.

## Particle Trails

Each pet supports configurable particle trail animations that play as it follows you. Trails are defined per-pet in `pets.yml` and can be customized to match the pet's theme.

## Unlocking Pets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Earn Lucky Blocks">
    Players receive pet unlock lucky blocks through normal gameplay (mining milestones, events, etc.) or via admin gifts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Your Lucky Block">
    Use the lucky block from your inventory to roll for a pet unlock. The chances for each pet are configured in `pets.yml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip Your New Pet">
    Once unlocked, open `/pets` and equip your new companion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Admin commands:**

Give a pet unlock item to a player directly:

```
/pets give <player> <pet-id>
```

**Example:**

```
/pets give Steve boomber
```

You can also grant access to a specific pet without an unlock item by assigning the permission:

```
jpprison.pet.<id>
```

This permission defaults to **op only**.

## Configuration

Edit `plugins/JPPrison/pets.yml` to adjust:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| `levels` | XP thresholds and boost values per level |
| `boosts` | Per-economy multiplier values |
| `armor` | Available armor sets and color codes |
| `particles` | Trail animation types and settings |
| `lucky-block-chances` | Drop weight for each pet in lucky blocks |

```yaml
# Example pets.yml snippet
pets:
  boomber:
    free: true
    levels:
      1:
        xp-required: 0
        boosts:
          tokens: 0.05
          money: 0.03
      10:
        xp-required: 50000
        boosts:
          tokens: 0.25
          money: 0.15
    particles:
      type: FLAME
      density: 5
    armor:
      helmet-color: "FF4500"
      chestplate-color: "FF4500"
```
