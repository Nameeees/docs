Momentum rewards non-stop mining with a combo multiplier. The longer you mine without pausing, the higher your multiplier grows — boosting all economy earnings and XP until you stop. It's a simple mechanic with a powerful payoff: stay in the zone and the prison pays you back for it.

## How Combos Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Mining">
    Every block you break increments your combo counter by 1. Your multiplier begins scaling immediately from the first block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the Chain Going">
    Your combo multiplier scales with the counter — the higher your block count, the stronger the boost applied to all autosell earnings and XP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Don't Stop">
    If you stop mining for longer than the configured reset timeout (default: 5 seconds), your combo resets back to **1×**. Even a brief pause costs you the entire chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hit Milestones">
    Reaching configured milestone combo counts triggers celebrations and awards bonus payouts — keep pushing toward the next milestone for extra rewards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Milestone Celebrations

When you reach a configured milestone combo count (for example, 100, 500, or 1000 blocks mined in a row), Momentum triggers a celebration:

- A **chat announcement** broadcasts your achievement to the server (if enabled)
- **Particle effects** fire around your character
- A **bonus reward payout** is deposited directly — extra money, tokens, or whatever your server has configured

Milestone thresholds, announcements, and bonus values are all defined in `config.yml` under the `momentum` section.

<Info>
  Milestones are one-time-per-session events — reaching 100 blocks fires the 100-block milestone once, but it won't fire again until your combo resets and you reach 100 again.
</Info>

## Viewing Your Combo

Your current combo counter and active multiplier are displayed **on-screen at all times** while mining. Depending on your server's configuration this appears as:

- An **actionbar message** below your hotbar (default)
- A **scoreboard line** on the side of your screen

You can also check your current multiplier at any time via `/prison` or by looking at the lore on your pickaxe, which updates dynamically.

## Configuration

Add or edit the `momentum` block in `plugins/JPPrison/config.yml`:

```yaml
momentum:
  enabled: true
  reset-timeout-seconds: 5
  milestones:
    - combo: 100
      bonus-multiplier: 0.1
      announce: true
    - combo: 500
      bonus-multiplier: 0.25
      announce: true
    - combo: 1000
      bonus-multiplier: 0.50
      announce: true
    - combo: 5000
      bonus-multiplier: 1.00
      announce: true
```

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| `enabled` | Turn Momentum on or off globally |
| `reset-timeout-seconds` | Seconds of inactivity before the combo resets |
| `milestones[].combo` | Block count that triggers this milestone |
| `milestones[].bonus-multiplier` | Extra multiplier awarded at this milestone |
| `milestones[].announce` | Whether to broadcast the milestone to the whole server |

<Tip>
  Combine Momentum with Timed Boosts and Pets for maximum earnings during long mining sessions. A high combo multiplier stacks with pet economy boosts and active timed boosts — the three systems together can multiply your baseline earnings several times over.
</Tip>
